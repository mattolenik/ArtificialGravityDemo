Scope: R2 of [2026-10-27-terrain-slice-1-followups.md](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-terrain-slice-1-followups.md). Read-only review against the code on `terrain` at c6240c7; one headless probe script run outside the project tree (it wrote and removed files under `user://`, touched no project file).

## Verdict: Ready with fixes

The technical claims hold against the code: the octave loop, break condition, weights and their sum, the rock multiplier, the 64-byte block and its packing, the std430 offsets of the proposed 112-byte block, the far-window seam arithmetic, the fidelity of the mismatch method to the bake, `regenerate()` against `move_to`, and old-file loading. Two medium defects (a crash in the driver's `_ready` as written; the mismatch `max` polluted at a window edge) and a handful of low ones, none structural.

## Defects

1. **Plan line 130 (Task 1 Step 3, `_ready`): connects both windows' `tiles_generated` before the windows exist.** `Terrain.window` is created in `Terrain._ready` ([terrain.gd:64-67](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/terrain.gd)), which runs inside the driver's `add_child(main)` and may be suspended at `await station.ready` ([terrain.gd:61-62](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/terrain.gd)) when `add_child` returns; `far.window` likewise ([far_terrain.gd:28-29](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/far_terrain.gd)). In `_ready`, before or after `add_child`, `terrain.window` is null: the connect is a null-instance error on every run. `smoke_run.gd` connects them in `_route` after `collider_live`, together with `_listening = true` ([smoke_run.gd:80-82](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/smoke_run.gd)), so lists and dispatches pair up for `_per_tile` ([smoke_run.gd:238-244](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/smoke_run.gd)). Fix: move the two `tiles_generated` connects into `_route` item 2 and say the `timed` collection starts at the same moment (gate as `_listening`); `_ready` keeps `collider_live` and `timed` only.

2. **Plan lines 59 and 133 (`row_mismatch` and `_mismatch`): a NaN cell's neighbours are kept, so `max` is the macro-to-detail gap where the patch leaves the window, not the chord error.** The bake takes cells outside the wanted set from the bilinear macro surface ([collider_bake.glsl:50-65](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/shaders/collider_bake.glsl)); the driver NaN-skips those cells but compares the last in-window vertex `k` against collider samples that sit on the chord from `k` to the macro point `k+1`, so vertex `k` carries a share of the whole detail-vs-macro difference (up to the 4..32 m octaves' sum, metres on rock). At the rock altitude the patch spans ±167 cells (128 m / 0.767 m) and the window's margin spinward of the viewer's tile is `256 - u` cells ([terrain_window.gd:237-250](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/terrain_window.gd)), so a site with cell offset `u > 89` in its tile has from one to 78 such columns per row; one column per row moves `n` by 0.3 %, which Review Focus 5's "a much smaller n" does not catch, while `max` in all 257 rows is polluted. Fix: in `row_mismatch` skip a vertex whose neighbour on either side is NaN as well (one condition), change the test at plan line 101 to `e2.size() == e.size() - 3`, and in Step 4 state the exact expected `n` rather than "much smaller".

3. **Plan lines 19 and 27 (decisions 2 and 6): "a world generated after this change pins its values in its own file" is false for default-valued fields.** `ResourceSaver` omits properties equal to the script default: the default world's `params.tres` on disk is `script = ExtResource(...)` and nothing else, though every field is `@export`ed ([world_params.gd:6-38](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/world_params.gd)); the probe saved a `WorldParams` with `seed = 1` and `h_sea = 401.0` and the file holds `h_sea` only. So a world carries the noise knobs only when they differ from the defaults at save time, and Task 3's change of the defaults retunes every default-valued world, including the user's. The "a world must carry the noise it was made with" argument for putting the knobs in `WorldParams` does not hold through this mechanism (it still holds that `WorldParams` is where the knobs belong and reach the shader). Fix: drop the pinning sentence from both decisions; say the defaults apply to every world whose file omits them. No task changes. This matters for the edit-deltas plan (R1), which regenerates unedited cells against the saved params.

4. **Plan lines 143 and 231 (Task 1 Step 4, Task 2 Step 6): `_surface.png` cannot show or hide per-cell grain.** `_dump` scales the whole window's heights min..max onto 8-bit grey ([debug_window_dump.gd:28-33, 44](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/debug_window_dump.gd)); a mountain window spans hundreds of metres, so one level is metres and the 0.38 m per-cell noise and the 0.76 m staircase are under one level. The expectations "`before_rock_surface.png` showing per-cell grain" and "`after_rock_surface.png` shows no per-cell grain" cannot be met by that file. Fix: delete those two expectations; the `_eye.png` views are the grain check (the plan already says so for `after_rock_eye.png`). Keep the dump as the before/after artefact.

5. **Plan line 177 (Task 2 Interfaces, `_push_constants`): a list longer than its slot is unspecified.** "a list shorter than its slot padded with zeros" says nothing about seven octaves or six amplitudes; an unguarded loop over `list.size()` writes `detail_octaves[6]` at offset 88 over `region_amp[0]`, and `region_amp[5]` at 108 over the pad, with no error (the buffer is still 112 bytes). Review Focus 2 names only the short case. Fix: add "longer truncated" to the Interfaces line and one assertion to the test (a 7-entry `detail_octaves` leaves `decode_float(88)` equal to `detail_region_amp[0]`).

6. **Plan lines 3 and 260-277 (Task 3): an interactive task under a header that mandates subagent-driven execution.** Steps 1 and 2 need the user to name candidates and look at PNGs; a subagent cannot do either, and the commit message at line 287 has placeholders. Fix: one line at the top of Task 3 saying it runs in the main session with the user, after Tasks 1 and 2 are merged, and that its only code change is the defaults.

7. **Plan line 17 (decision 1): "raises the near-only share ... from 0.067 (the 4 m octave) to 0.22" reads as if 0.067 were today's figure.** Today the near-only share is (0.125 + 0.0625 + 0.03125) / 1.96875 = 0.111 (the 4, 2 and 1 m octaves, [detail.glsl:187-203](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/shaders/detail.glsl)); 0.067 = 0.125 / 1.875 is the proposal's, 0.22 the alternative's. The arithmetic behind each number is right. Fix: "today 0.111; this plan 0.067; the alternative 0.22".

## Strengths

- Every statement about today's shader is right: six octaves 32..1 m, `if (float(s) < pc.cell) break` so the 8 m window runs 32, 16, 8 and the 1 m window all six ([detail.glsl:187-191](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/shaders/detail.glsl)); weights `s / 32` summing to 1.96875 with that divisor ([detail.glsl:201-203](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/shaders/detail.glsl)); `AMP = {1.5, 1.5, 4, 8, 4}` and `1.0 + 2.0 * exposed` with `exposed` fading over the last 1 m of cover ([detail.glsl:49, 300-301](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/shaders/detail.glsl)); the 64-byte block with `pad` at 60 and GDScript encoding through 56 ([detail.glsl:30-45](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/shaders/detail.glsl), [terrain_window.gd:295-314](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/terrain_window.gd)). The blends 0.156 / 0.844 and the 0.38 m and 0.76 m peaks follow from `u = g + 0.5`.
- The proposed layout is correct std430: scalar arrays have 4-byte stride and 4-byte alignment, so `rock_roughness` 60, `octave[6]` 64..87, `region_amp[5]` 88..107, `pad` 108, size 112; under Vulkan's 128-byte guarantee and Metal's `setBytes` limit. Godot checks the buffer size against the reflected block size on every `compute_list_set_push_constant`, which is what Review Focus 3 says.
- The seam argument is sound: with `norm` the sum of all six weights the far window's output is scaled by 1.96875 / 1.875 = 1.05 and nothing else, the same factor the near window's 32..8 m terms get; and zero weights for 2 m and 1 m touch only the 1 m window, since the far window breaks at `s = 4`.
- The mismatch method mirrors the bake: `half_n = ceil(asin(129 / (R - h_sea - h_mtn)) / dtheta) = 204`, 409 samples per row at cell centres, rows at cell centres (`z = -L/2 + cell.y + 0.5`, [collider_patch.gd:121, 133-134](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/collider_patch.gd)), the mapping `((R - h) sin phi, h + 2 (R - h) sin^2(phi/2))` ([collider_bake.glsl:82-86](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/shaders/collider_bake.glsl)), upper envelope, and the slot addressing `(c / TILE % slots) * TILE + c % TILE` equals `posmod(c, 512)` for a 4-slot window. The tile shader bends vertices with the same form ([cylinder_tile.gdshaderinc:61-67](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/shaders/cylinder_tile.gdshaderinc)), so drawn vertices lie on the bake polyline and the in-row max is at a drawn vertex, as decision 4 says.
- `Cyl.bake_row(h, dtheta, R, 1.0, 257)` matches the real signature ([cyl.gd:32](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/cyl.gd)). The two headless cases pin the function: on the 45° slope the only error is the hull's sagitta over one chord (about 6e-5 m) plus float32 rounding of the collider output (6e-5 m), under the 1e-3 bound; the ±0.2 m zigzag at 0.767 m spacing gives a worst chord error of 0.4 / 0.767 × 0.5 ≈ 0.26 m when a peak falls midway between integer x, inside (0.05, 0.4].
- `regenerate()` as described works: `move_to` marks a tile dirty whenever `_resident[slot] != tile` ([terrain_window.gd:261-265](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/terrain_window.gd)), so a cleared residency dispatches all 16 and emits `tiles_generated`; push constants are built per dispatch ([terrain_window.gd:285-287](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/terrain_window.gd)). The plan does not forget the collider: `patch.rebuild(window, patch.center.x, patch.center.y)` then `await patch.live` is the right sequence, and the bake is queued after the dispatch on the render thread as the startup path already does ([terrain.gd:75-77](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/terrain.gd)).
- The old-file test is valid headless and proves what it claims (probe: a `.tres` with `seed = 7` only loads as `WorldParams` with `seed 7`, `radius 3200`); `PackedFloat32Array ==` compares contents; the packing test's fixed-field assertions already hold against today's `_push_constants` (3, 384, 1.0, 10.0). The two GDScript constructs the tests rely on (`var v` after `for v: Vector3 in` loops; `for x in 64: for y in 128:` with an indented block) compile.
- The light-bar parking is right: `theta` is a plain var that `_process` drifts by `TAU / day_length` per second ([light_bar.gd:24, 62-65](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/light_bar.gd)), so `theta = site`, `day_length = 1e9` holds noon over the site.
- Against R2: the followups quote the loop as `s < 2.0 * pc.cell`, which is not today's code; the plan describes today's correctly. R2 names both the 1 m and 2 m octaves as the per-cell noise ("at or past Nyquist"), and the 2 m octave sampled at `f = 0.25 / 0.75` is a 2-cell staircase at twice the 1 m amplitude, so dropping both is the reading consistent with R2's own reasoning; the plan says why the literal "under 2 × cell" per window loses (it would drop the far window's 8 m octave) and measures the 1 m-only case anyway (`no1m`). Knobs in `WorldParams`, the before/after measurement, the Vulkan budget check and the user's tuning pass are all there.
- Interfaces are named identically across tasks; the Review Focus lines each have a pin; the plan's length is about that of the code it describes.

## Not verified

- The GLSL change compiles and reflects 112 bytes on Metal and MoltenVK (`float octave[6]`, the runtime-indexed `pc.region_amp[id]`): there is no RenderingDevice headless and a windowed run was out of scope. The std430 offsets are right by the rules; the plan's fallback for the dynamic index is sensible.
- The claim that the 2 m staircase is the larger share of the mismatch, and the expected before/after magnitudes (spawn p99 under 1 cm, rock about half): these are what the plan measures.
- That the new statistic is the one slice 1 reported (18 cm max, 8 cm p99): that measurement's method and site are not in the repository (nothing in `docs/terrain-research.md` or the slice 1 plan mentions it), so the plan's own caveat that `before` is the baseline for this method is the right stance.
- That `rock_site`'s highest bare macro cell has undamped noise: a summit usually does, but the shader damps amplitude by 10× on slopes over 45..55° ([detail.glsl:302](/Users/matt/ArtificialGravityDemo/godot/terrain/shaders/detail.glsl)), so a highest cell on a ridge face would understate the rock figures; `--at` covers it.
- The headless suite was not run; nothing in the review depended on its current output.
